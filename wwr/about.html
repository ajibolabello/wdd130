Splash Whitewater Rafting

At Splash, we believe the best memories are made on the river. Our experienced guides bring the gear, local knowledge, and care for a safe adventure your whole crew will remember.

History

Splash began guiding river trips in 1995 with one raft, a few friends, and a love for the water. Over the years, we have grown into a family-run team that welcomes first-time paddlers and returning adventurers alike. Our promise remains the same: share the river responsibly and make every trip count.
